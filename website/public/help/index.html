Getting started

Open the Scan tab, point the camera at an insect and take the photo. Critterboard identifies it on your phone, adds it to your Dex and gives you XP.

Identification needs a regional pack. Go to Me → Brains, pick your region and tap Get. The pack holds the species list and the BugNet vision model (about 88 MB) and is downloaded once.

Regional packs and the offline map

Only one regional pack is active at a time. It decides which species Critterboard can recognise and which map you see. Right now Central Europe is available; other regions are marked soon.

The offline map comes with the pack (about 56 MB, all of Europe at low detail). The Map tab stays locked until it is downloaded. After that it works without any connection and shows pins where you caught things.

  • Download packs on Wi-Fi: the model and the map are tens of megabytes.
  • Your location is used only to centre the map and place pins. It is never sent anywhere unless you turn on location sharing.

XP, rarity, quests, badges and streaks

  • XP and levels: every new species earns XP. Rarer species (common → uncommon → rare → epic → legendary) earn more.
  • Quests: weekly goals such as “Find a Legendary”. Open Me → Quests.
  • Badges: start at zero and unlock as you play. Tap one to see how to earn it.
  • Streaks: catch something every day. You earn a freeze every 7 days of activity (up to three) and it is spent automatically if you miss a day.

Network, leaderboard and friends

Critterboard works fully offline and the network switch is off by default. Turn it on in Me → Brains only if you want the online features.

  • Leaderboard: opt-in. Your name and XP appear only if you switch it on.
  • Friends: follow other trainers from the Ranks tab. Friends see your public profile only.
  • Share spotting locations: optional. Public pins are blurred by about 500 m.
  • Crash reports: optional and anonymous.

There are no accounts. Your phone creates a random private key on first use; it is how the server recognises your device. If you reinstall the app or wipe your data you start over as a new trainer.

Chat and the guides

The chat guides (Prof. Larva, Dr. Snail and the research assistant) run on your phone with the Gemma 4 E2B model. It is an optional download (about 3.1 GB, Wi-Fi recommended) in Me → Brains. Without it, chat is off and everything else still works.

Troubleshooting

  • No match or low confidence: see the photo tips below, and check that you installed the pack for your region.
  • The Map tab is locked: download the active region in Me → Brains. You need a connection once.
  • The map or a download fails: check your connection and try again; interrupted downloads are discarded, never half-installed.
  • Location not detected: allow location for Critterboard in iOS or Android Settings, then reopen the Map tab.
  • Change the language: Me → Brains → Language. It changes the app, species names and the guides.

Taking a good photo

🔆
Brighter light
Move into the sun or use the flash. Shadow eats detail.
🔍
Fill the frame
Get within 20 cm and crop tight on the body.
🌿
Plain backdrop
Less foliage clutter helps the model focus.
📐
Side profile
Wings and legs visible from the side are easiest.

FAQ

Does Critterboard work without internet?

Yes. Vision, chat, and the species database all run on your phone. The only reason to turn on Network access is to share to the leaderboard or post sightings publicly.

Why is my ID confidence low?

Usually one of three things: the photo is blurry, the bug fills less than 1/4 of the frame, or you're outside an installed regional pack. Try a closer crop, brighter light, and a side profile.

How do streak freezes work?

You earn one freeze every 7 days of activity, capped at three banked. If you miss a day, the freeze auto-spends to keep your streak alive. You don't have to do anything.

Can I use Critterboard if I'm colorblind?

Yes. All confidence and rarity cues double-encode with shape and label. There's a high-contrast pass in development; ping us if a specific screen is hurting you.

Where do reference images come from?

iNaturalist (CC-BY-NC) for photos; GBIF for ranges. We don't train on your photos — model updates ship from our side only.

How do I delete my data?

Scroll down to "Wipe everything." It nukes your dex, sightings, settings, and on-device models. Followers and leaderboard entries go too. There is no undo.

Your data

Everything lives on your phone: your Dex, sightings, photos and settings. There is no account, no ad tracking and no analytics unless you opt into crash reports.

  • Export: Me → Help lets you export your Dex (JSON), sightings (CSV), a GBIF-ready occurrence file (Darwin Core) or everything at once.
  • Wipe: “Wipe everything” in the same screen removes your Dex, sightings, settings and downloaded models. Followers and leaderboard entries go too. There is no undo.
  • Photos and models: reference photos come from iNaturalist and GBIF ranges. We never train on your photos.
