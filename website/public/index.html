How it works

A field guide in your pocket.

Critterboard uses a small AI model that runs on your phone. Photos are never uploaded anywhere, so it works just as well deep in the woods as it does at home.

Each region gets its own model, trained only on the species you're likely to find there. That keeps it small and accurate.

🧠
Small and fast Runs smoothly on everyday phones, not just the latest flagships.
🗺️
One pack per region Each pack covers one region's insects, so it stays small and knows the species you'll actually see.
📦

Download once, before you go ~50–100 MB

Download the AI pack for your region once, ideally at home on Wi-Fi. After that, Critterboard works without a connection: in the woods, in a meadow, or anywhere with no signal.
