# Everyday species people meet at home or in the garden, always included in
# the species list whatever their observation rank. Research-grade counts
# undersell these: they are hard to confirm from photos, so few observations
# reach research grade. Most are cosmopolitan, so stream_obs.sh takes their
# observations worldwide, not just from Europe. One Latin name per line.
# A species with too few CC0/CC-BY photos is still dropped by
# select_commercial.py (--min-photos); it prints which ones.
#
# Names and photo counts checked on iNaturalist (Oct 2026). Left out, under
# 100 usable photos worldwide: thrips (Frankliniella occidentalis, Thrips
# tabaci), onion fly (Delia antiqua), carrot fly (Chamaepsila rosae), flea
# beetles (Phyllotreta), Tetramorium ants. Fruit fly has ~150: expect it weak.
# Showy garden species (ladybirds, hoverflies, shield bugs, chafers, garden
# spider) rank in on their own.
